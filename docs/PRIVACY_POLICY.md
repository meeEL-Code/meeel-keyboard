# meeKeyboard — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 21 September 2026**

## Overview

meeKeyboard is a keyboard application developed by mee Innovations
for Android. This document explains what data meeKeyboard accesses
and how it is handled.

## Data We Collect

**We do not collect any personal data.**

meeKeyboard is designed to work entirely on your device. There is no
account, no sign-in, and no cloud sync. Nothing you type is ever
transmitted to any server.

## What Stays on Your Device

The following information is stored locally on your device only:

- **Learned words** — words you type frequently, saved to help your
  suggestions improve. Stored in the app's private storage.
- **Clipboard history** — text you copy while meeKeyboard is active,
  stored in the app's private storage.
- **Settings and preferences** — theme, language, background, and
  sound settings. Stored in the app's private storage.
- **Custom background image** (optional) — if you choose an image from
  your gallery, it is copied to the app's private storage.

None of this data leaves your device. Uninstalling the app deletes
all of it.

## Permissions

meeKeyboard requests the following:

- **Vibration** — for haptic feedback on key taps.
- **Internet** — this is a development artifact; the current version
  does not transmit any data.

## Clipboard Access

meeKeyboard reads the system clipboard only to show you its contents
in the built-in clipboard panel (when you tap the clipboard icon).
Clipboard contents are never sent anywhere.

## Children's Privacy

meeKeyboard does not collect any information from anyone, including
children under 13. It is safe for use by all ages.
