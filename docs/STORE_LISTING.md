## Full Description (4000 chars max)

**meeKeyboard is a clean, fast, private keyboard for Android.**

Made for everyday typing — with English and Bengali, emoji, clipboard history, word suggestions, and full customization. No ads. No tracking. No account.

### Why meeKeyboard?

**🎯 Simple and fast**
A clean layout with rounded keys, ripple press effect, and haptic feedback. Just start typing — everything you need is one tap away.

**🔒 Truly private**
Your typing stays on your device. We don't collect anything. No analytics. No ads. No servers.

**🌐 Two languages, one keyboard**
Switch between English and Bengali with a single tap on the globe key. Both layouts are hand-tuned for comfortable typing.

**🧠 Learns your words**
Type a word a few times and meeKeyboard remembers it — suggestions get smarter the more you use it.

**✂️ Built-in clipboard**
A full clipboard history right inside the keyboard. Long-press to pin, delete, or clear. No third-party app needed.

**😀 500+ emojis**
All the emojis you need, organized by category with a Recent row that remembers your favorites.

**📚 Suggestions + auto-correct**
Real-time word suggestions above the keys. Auto-correct catches typos as you type — and a simple backspace restores your original if you disagree.

**🎨 Themes and backgrounds**
Dark, Light, Blue, or your own image from the gallery. Six gradient presets. Make it yours.

**⌨️ Every feature you expect**
- Long-press letter for numbers and symbols
- Double-tap space for period
- Long-press backspace for fast delete
- Shift with caps lock
- Cursor arrows
- Auto-capitalize
- Sound toggle
- Vibration feedback
- Bengali দাঁড়ি (।) shortcut
- ⌫, ⏎, and space always within reach

### What we don't do

- No ads
- No analytics
- No account
- No cloud sync
- No personal data collection
